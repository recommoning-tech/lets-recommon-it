## Summary
Edited volume that extends commons analysis from natural resources to knowledge and information, including digital libraries, open access, and open source software.

## Why it's useful here
Bridges Ostrom's natural-resource work to digital infrastructure. Shows where the analogy holds (governance, free-riding on maintenance) and where it breaks (information is non-rivalrous, but bandwidth, storage, and admin time are not).

## Key ideas
- Institutional Analysis and Development (IAD) framework applied to knowledge
- Digital commons face enclosure through law and technology, not scarcity
