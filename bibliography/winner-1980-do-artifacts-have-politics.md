## Summary
Argues that technologies can embody political arrangements, either by design (the low Long Island overpasses example, now disputed) or because some technologies inherently require certain social structures.

## Why it's useful here
A short, citable foundation for the claim that architecture choices (centralized vs. federated) are governance choices.

## Key ideas
- Technologies as forms of order
- Inherently political technologies vs. politically designed ones
