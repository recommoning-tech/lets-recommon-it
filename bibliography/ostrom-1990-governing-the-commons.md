## Summary
Field studies of fisheries, irrigation systems, and forests show that communities often manage shared resources well without privatization or state control. Ostrom distills eight design principles common to long-lasting commons institutions.

## Why it's useful here
The core framework for governing a shared server, network, or service. The eight principles can be applied directly to admin rights, account policies, and dispute resolution.

## Key ideas
- Rebuts the "tragedy of the commons" as inevitable
- Eight design principles: clear boundaries; rules fit local conditions; collective-choice arrangements; monitoring; graduated sanctions; conflict resolution; recognized right to organize; nested enterprises
- Common-pool resources vs. public and private goods
