## Summary
Describes how platforms decay: first they serve users, then they exploit users to serve business customers, then they exploit everyone to extract value for shareholders. Identifies the policy failures that allow it.

## Why it's useful here
A widely recognized vocabulary for why communities should own their infrastructure, and a test for whether a self-hosted alternative is resistant to the same decay.

## Key ideas
- Three-stage platform decay
- Constraints that once disciplined platforms: competition, regulation, interoperability, worker power
