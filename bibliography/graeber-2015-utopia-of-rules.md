## Summary
Essays on how market societies multiply bureaucracy, how bureaucratic power depends on implicit violence, and how technological progress has shifted from poetic to bureaucratic technologies.

## Why it's useful here
A lens for terms of service, content moderation, and account lockouts as bureaucracy without recourse. It also warns that community governance can turn into the same thing.

## Key ideas
- "Iron law of liberalism": market reforms increase bureaucracy
- Poetic vs. bureaucratic technologies
- Structural violence and "dead zones of the imagination"
