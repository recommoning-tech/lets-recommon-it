## Summary
The theory companion to *A Pattern Language*. Describes a "quality without a name" found in living places, and argues that it comes from shared pattern languages and gradual, piecemeal growth.

## Why it's useful here
Supports growing community infrastructure incrementally, in response to real use, over large up-front builds.

## Key ideas
- "Quality without a name"
- Piecemeal growth over master plans
