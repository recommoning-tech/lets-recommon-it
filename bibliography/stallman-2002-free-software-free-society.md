## Summary
Essays defining free software through four freedoms, explaining copyleft and the GPL, and arguing that software freedom is an ethical issue, not just a practical one.

## Why it's useful here
The ethical counterpart to Raymond. Copyleft is a legal tool for keeping a commons from being enclosed, and it informs the choice of code license for this repo.

## Key ideas
- Four freedoms: run, study/modify, redistribute, distribute modified versions
- Copyleft as commons protection
