## Summary
Uses archaeological and anthropological evidence to challenge the idea that growing societies inevitably become hierarchical states. Early humans experimented with many political arrangements, including seasonal and reversible ones.

## Why it's useful here
Counters "it can't scale" objections. Social arrangements are choices, and communities can experiment with governance structures for shared tech.

## Key ideas
- Three basic freedoms: to move, to disobey, to reorganize social relations
- Political self-consciousness of early societies
