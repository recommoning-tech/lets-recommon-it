## Summary
Studies how open source projects actually work today. Many are maintained by one or a few people facing heavy demands from users who don't contribute.

## Why it's useful here
An empirical correction to the bazaar ideal. Most projects aren't communities, which matters when choosing self-hosted software that a community will depend on.

## Key ideas
- Project types: federations, clubs, toys, stadiums
- Maintenance burden and attention as scarce resources
