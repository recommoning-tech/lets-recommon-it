## Summary
Argues that networked information technology enables "commons-based peer production," large-scale cooperation outside markets and firms, as seen in Wikipedia and free software.

## Why it's useful here
An economic and legal case for digital commons, and a good counterpoint to the platform-dominated internet that came after.

## Key ideas
- Commons-based peer production
- Nonmarket production in the networked information economy
