## Summary
Bookchin's major work of social ecology. Argues that the domination of nature stems from human hierarchy, and traces how hierarchy emerged and could be dissolved.

## Why it's useful here
Supplies the broader theory behind the blog's preference for non-hierarchical, federated systems over centralized platforms.

## Key ideas
- Social ecology: ecological problems are social problems
- Hierarchy as historically contingent, not natural
