## Summary
A practical theory of commoning built from observed patterns of social life, peer governance, and provisioning in real commons. It explicitly uses an Alexander-style pattern language.

## Why it's useful here
Connects Ostrom to Alexander directly. It is the closest existing model for the wiki's pattern language and is written for practitioners.

## Key ideas
- Commoning as a verb (practice, not resource)
- Triad: social life, peer governance, provisioning
- Pattern language of commoning
