## Summary
Critiques tech-industry transportation schemes (ride-hailing, autonomous vehicles, Hyperloop) as serving the wealthy and industry rather than public needs, and argues for public transit and better urban design.

## Why it's useful here
A case study in how tech solutionism displaces public, collective solutions, and an argument that applies to ICT as well. See also Marx's podcast *Tech Won't Save Us*.

## Key ideas
- Tech solutions often reproduce existing inequities
- Collective/public provision over individualized products
