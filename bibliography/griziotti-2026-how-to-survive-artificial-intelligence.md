## Summary
Rejects the three dominant AI narratives (doom, salvation, transhumanist enhancement) as ways of avoiding a catastrophe already underway. Griziotti treats AI as a relational reality rather than "AI vs. the human." It is a web of people, algorithms, hardware, capital, and politics, analyzed through Barad's diffraction, Simondon, and his own concept of neurocapitalism. He argues that AI is the operative core of a "technofascist megamachine": hardware-centered, ecologically devastating, financially circular, and already deployed in surveillance and war. The epilogue looks to Gen Z revolts and to "fragile, partial, interstitial" alternatives: federated platforms, data cooperatives, mesh networks, small local models, and decolonial datasets.

## Why it's useful here
- **Foundational text for the `ai` thread.** It places AI inside the same relations of extraction and control that the rest of the blog addresses.
- **Its three directions match this blog's work directly.**
  - *Seizing infrastructures from monopoly:* Mastodon, data co-ops, Indigenous data sovereignty.
  - *Breaking ecological devastation:* lightweight local models on community devices, mesh networks, situated AIs on small datasets that federate while staying autonomous.
  - *Incorporating subaltern knowledge:* decolonial datasets, Masakhane.
- **The question it leaves open is this blog's question:** "How can these practices scale without reproducing centralization?" This is where IAD and polycentricity come in.
- **Hardware is central again.** AI's concentration in GPUs and data centers resembles the mainframe era. That makes community-owned compute a political question, not just a technical one.
- **Models critical use.** Griziotti co-wrote the essay with a chatbot and reports on it honestly ("the work of discernment is irreplaceable"). That is relevant to how this project itself uses AI.

## Key ideas
- **Neurocapitalism:** emotions, cognition, and affect become both raw material for value and variables of social control
- **Meta-technics vs. meta-automation:** the human capacity to invent new techniques vs. ML as the "automation of automation" (after Pasquinelli)
- **"Everything is a hallucination"** (after Colin Fraser): LLM output is always probabilistic, so the problem is using a probabilistic tool for deterministic tasks
- **World-technology** (after Caronia): LLMs are not a prosthesis but an ecosystem that reshapes knowledge and epistemic authority
- **Comfort as control** (after Mhalla): "The true opposite of democracy today is not dictatorship; it is comfort"
- **Computational necropolitics:** automated decisions about whom to surveil, exclude, and kill (Lavender, Where's Daddy?, Palantir)
- **Molecular strategy:** accumulate partial victories and points of friction rather than storming a "Winter Palace"
