## Summary
Contrasts closed, centrally planned development (cathedral) with Linux's open, distributed model (bazaar). Became a founding text of the "open source" framing.

## Why it's useful here
Essential history of the open source movement. Also useful as a foil: it treats open source as a development method more than a social or governance commitment.

## Key ideas
- "Given enough eyeballs, all bugs are shallow" (Linus's Law)
- Release early, release often
