## Summary
Argues that Big Tech's power comes from high switching costs, and that mandated and adversarial interoperability would let users leave without losing their networks and data.

## Why it's useful here
Makes the policy and technical case for self-hosting and open protocols (ActivityPub, Matrix, email). Interoperability is what makes community-run services viable alongside platforms.

## Key ideas
- Switching costs as the source of lock-in
- Adversarial interoperability ("comcom": competitive compatibility)
- Enshittification, developed further in his 2025 book
