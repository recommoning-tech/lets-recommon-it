## Summary
A history of the Charter of the Forest alongside Magna Carta, showing how commoners' rights to subsistence were tied to political liberty, and how enclosure eroded both.

## Why it's useful here
Source of the "commoning" verb ("there is no commons without commoning"), which underlies the name *Recommon*. It supplies the historical framing for posts about enclosure of the digital commons.

## Key ideas
- Commoning as embedded in labor and practice
- Enclosure as a recurring process
