## Summary
Essays on the commons as a practice of social reproduction. Federici is critical of commons projects that ignore who does the care work.

## Why it's useful here
A direct warning for community tech: who patches the server at 2 a.m.? It shapes how guides treat maintenance and how governance posts distribute that labor.

## Key ideas
- Commons as social relations, not just resources
- Feminist critique of digital/knowledge commons that ignore reproduction
