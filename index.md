# The table

The table of threads and levels, and the pattern table, are generated from front matter and published on the website: <https://recommoning-tech.github.io/lets-recommon-it/>.

- Rows are threads; columns are levels of rules: guides (operational), governance (collective-choice and constitutional), theory (metaconstitutional). See [This repository is a commons](theory/this-repository-is-a-commons.md).
- A piece's row is the first entry in its `threads:` front matter; its column is its folder.
- A pattern's cell is its `thread` and `level`.
