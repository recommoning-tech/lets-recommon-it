## The idea
Most of our digital lives run on machines owned by someone else, and architecture is politics. This release makes the case for owning the machine, teaches you to set up a small, secure server, and then asks the harder question: once other people depend on it, who decides how it's run?

## Read in this order
1. **Theory:** [Who owns the machine?](../theory/who-owns-the-machine.md): Winner, Moglen, Franklin, Illich, and Doctorow on why infrastructure ownership matters
2. **Guide:** [Setting up your first server](../guides/first-server.md): Debian 13, SSH keys, firewall, automatic updates
3. **Governance:** [Who holds root?](../governance/who-holds-root.md): an IAD analysis of a shared server, ADICO rules, and a one-page charter template
