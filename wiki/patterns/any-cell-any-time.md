*Small contributions are welcome contributions.*

## Context
A site written collectively and organized as a table, with threads as rows and guides, governance, and theory as columns. Contributors have limited time.

## Problem
If the unit of contribution is large, such as a full set of theory, guide, and governance for one topic, only people with a lot of time can contribute. Finished work sits unpublished until the whole set is done, and the few people who can finish a set end up setting the direction for everyone.

## Forces
- Good places grow piecemeal, through many small acts of building and repair ([*The Timeless Way of Building*](../../bibliography/alexander-1979-timeless-way-of-building.md)).
- Much of open source depends on casual contributors who make one small change and move on ([*Working in Public*](../../bibliography/eghbal-2020-working-in-public.md)).
- What counts as a contribution, and who may make one, is a boundary and choice rule set at the collective-choice level ([*Understanding Institutional Diversity*](../../bibliography/ostrom-2005-understanding-institutional-diversity.md)).

## Solution
**Therefore, accept a contribution to any single cell of the table at any time, without waiting for the rest of its row.**

## Actions
- One guide, governance piece, theory essay, or pattern counts as a contribution.
- Anyone may propose a new thread. It's added through the project's collective-choice rules.
- Mark empty cells as open in the table.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** more people can contribute, and work is published sooner.
- **Negative:** rows fill unevenly, with three guides and no governance. The table makes that visible.
