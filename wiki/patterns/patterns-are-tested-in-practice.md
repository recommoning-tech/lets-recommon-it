## Context
A pattern language that grows out of essays and reading, as this one does.

## Problem
Patterns that come only from argument drift toward abstraction. They sound right but nobody has checked whether they work. Over time the language fills with good intentions instead of tested solutions.

## Discussion
Alexander's design method starts from fit: what actually goes wrong in real use ([*Notes on the Synthesis of Form*](../../bibliography/alexander-1964-notes-on-the-synthesis-of-form.md)). His patterns came from observing places that worked and places that failed, and he favored piecemeal growth through repair over master plans ([*The Timeless Way of Building*](../../bibliography/alexander-1979-timeless-way-of-building.md)). On this site, theory essays propose patterns, while guides and governance pieces are where they meet real servers and real groups.

## Therefore
**Every pattern is introduced in a theory essay and applied by name in at least one guide or governance piece. When a pattern doesn't fit what happens in practice, revise the pattern and note why.**
